Where Every Kadai Holds A Celebration

Located conveniently on the First Floor of Sathvikam along the bustling Nagole-Bandlaguda corridor, The Kadai Story was envisioned to bring the authentic depth of regional South Indian recipes and majestic North Indian culinary arts under one luxurious roof.

Authentic heat distribution preserves essential oils and unlocks layered aroma in every gravy.

Stone-Ground Spices & Zero Preservatives
